PQCrypt Rust benchmarks (release build, RustCrypto ml-kem 0.3 + aes-gcm 0.10 + x25519-dalek 2)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Run on:    Apple M4 Max, rustc stable (cargo --release), v1.3 (hybrid)
Date:      2026-05-21
Harness:   pqcrypt/rust/benches/bench_bin.rs (custom Instant::now() timing,
           3 iterations, best-of-3 reported; matches pqid/rust pattern).
Note:      RustCrypto ml-kem 0.3 is pure-Rust constant-time. ~40x faster than
           pure-Python kyber-py at keygen. AES-256-GCM uses aes-gcm 0.10
           portable backend on Apple Silicon (ARMv8 AES intrinsics deferred
           to v1.3 — see PQCK-00027). Hybrid suite 0x06 adds one X25519
           keygen + one DH per envelope (~30 us amortized).
