\section{Wire Format (Normative)}
\label{sec:wire-format}

Big-endian throughout. \textbf{Header: fixed 16 bytes. Body: variable.}

\begin{lstlisting}[language=Pseudocode]
Offset  Size  Field                                               AD?
------  ----  --------------------------------------------------  ----
0       6     TS         (timestamp_ms; provenance, not auth)     yes
6       1     VAR        (variant byte = 0x04 for v1)             yes
7       1     SUITE      (algorithm suite id; see sec04)          yes
8       2     CTR        (counter, monotonic per sender)          yes
10      2     KEM_CT_LEN (big-endian unsigned, <= 4096)            yes
12      4     AD_LEN     (length of caller-supplied AD)           yes
16      N     KEM_CT     (KEM ciphertext, N = KEM_CT_LEN)          yes
16+N    A     AD         (caller AD, A = AD_LEN)                   yes (carried)
16+N+A  12    AEAD_NONCE (derived from K_session; see sec07)       no  (derived)
...     M     AEAD_CT    (AEAD_E(K_aead, NONCE, PT))              ---
...     16    AEAD_TAG   (128-bit tag over header || KEM_CT || AD)---
...     32    COMMIT     (key-commitment tag; HMAC-SHA3-256)      ---
\end{lstlisting}

The first 9 bytes (\code{TS || VAR || CTR}) are layout-compatible with \pqid{} and \pqhash{}: a single \code{common/c/tessera\_header.h} pins those offsets across all three projects. Variant byte \code{0x04} is the first allocation from the previously-reserved \texttt{0x04--0xFE} range; \pqid{} and \pqhash{} decoders MUST reject \code{0x04}, and \pqcrypt{} decoders MUST reject every byte except \code{0x04} in v1.

\textbf{Length limits.} \code{KEM\_CT\_LEN} maximum 4096 bytes (ML-KEM-1024 produces 1568; the cap defends against malformed inputs causing oversized allocation). \code{AD\_LEN} maximum 65\,536 bytes. Plaintext maximum $2^{32}$ bytes (4 GiB); v1 is not a streaming format.

\textbf{Wire form for envelopes (binary).} v1 ships binary-only on the wire. Text encodings (Base64URL, Crockford Base32) are deferred to v1.1 because envelopes are variable-length and the encoder logic differs from \pqid{}'s and \pqhash{}'s fixed-length encoders. Callers that need text representations should wrap with standard Base64URL at the transport layer.
